`[ICLR'26]` **Four papers** accepted! Congrats to our students and collaborators! Especially congrats to **Junhan Zhu** (Westlake undergrad)—his first top-venue paper, done in sophomore summer, extends OBS pruning to diffusion models. Thanks to collaborators at ZJU, CASIA, and NUS!

- **OBS-Diff**: One-shot pruning for diffusion models. [[arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06751)] [[code](https://github.com/Alrightlone/OBS-Diff)]
- **MergeMix**: Unified augmentation for visual and multi-modal understanding. [[arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23479)]
- **RewardMap**: Multi-stage RL for fine-grained visual reasoning. [[arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02240)] [[code](https://github.com/fscdc/RewardMap)]
- **ARPG**: Autoregressive image generation with randomized parallel decoding. [[arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10568)] [[code](https://github.com/hp-l33/ARPG)]
