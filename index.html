Westlake ENCODE Lab

Efficient Neural COmputing and DEsign

We build efficient and reliable AI systems that are scalable and self-improving, driving both theoretical innovation and practical impact.

Our lab is led by Dr. Huan Wang, a tenure-track assistant professor in the School of Engineering at Westlake University.
