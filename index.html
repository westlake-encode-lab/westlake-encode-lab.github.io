Westlake ENCODE Lab

ENCODE Lab Family

About Us:
The ENCODE Lab (Efficient Neural COmputing and DEsign) is led by Dr. Huan Wang, a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor in the School of Engineering at Westlake University. We focus on building efficient and reliable AI systems that are scalable and self-improving, driving both theoretical innovation and practical impact.

Research Focus:
Our research centers on Efficient AI, Multimodal AI, and Generative AI.

Join Us:
We are actively recruiting Ph.D. students, Research Assistants, Visiting Students, and Postdocs. Please read the Admission Guide for openings and how to apply, and email us at encodelab@westlake.edu.cn.
